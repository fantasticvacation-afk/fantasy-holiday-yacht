🎯 Core Requirements

  • 65-meter-plus superyacht accommodating up to 22 guests and28 service staff
  • Michelin-starred chef providing fully customized multi-national cuisine
  • Private dive instructors and water sports team supporting deep-sea exploration
  • Multilingual concierge team (English, /FR/IT/DE/Western languages) with barrier-free communication
  • 12 countries24 berthing coordination and curated shore excursions across multiple ports

  • Flexible deployment for business hospitality scenarios and VIPProtection
