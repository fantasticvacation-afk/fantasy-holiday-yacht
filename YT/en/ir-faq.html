Fantastic VacationWhat is Fantastic Vacation's core business?

Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co.focuses on four core business segments: global luxury yacht customization & brokerage, Charter Routes operations、Yacht Managementmanagement services, and an exclusive membership system. The company represents Ferretti、Feadship、Lürssenand other global top shipyard resources, with an owned and partnered fleet exceeding 50，total managed assets value of exceeding 45billion RMB, serving over 200+ HNW clients.

What are the company's competitive moats and advantages?

The company's core competitive moats include: (1)a deeply embedded network of top shipyard resources, serving as exclusive or preferred agent for multiple leading international brands;(2)fifteen years of industry expertise accumulated know-howand client trust, with a repurchase rate of 65%+；(3)custom, charter, management, and membership—the four-wheel drive business synergy, with client LTV exceeding 1,500 million RMB；(4)an operational network spanning six global hub cities, delivering consistently high-quality service to clients worldwide.
