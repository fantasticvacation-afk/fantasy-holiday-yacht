Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd Wins 2026 Asia Best Yacht Service Provider Award

At the prestigious 2026 Asia Yachting Awards held at Marina Bay Sands, Singapore, Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd was honoured as Asia's Best Yacht Service Provider of the Year, standing out among 86 nominated enterprises from 12 countries. The award recognises the company's outstanding achievements in yacht customisation, charter operations, and exclusive membership services over the past year. Organised jointly by the Asia Yachting Industry Association (AYIA) and Asia Boating magazine, the award is widely regarded as the most authoritative and influential industry accolade in the Asia-Pacific yachting sector.

During the award ceremony, Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd Founder and CEO Hanson Lin delivered a keynote address, sharing the brand's philosophy of 'centering on the client, with the ocean as our radius.' He noted that over the past three years, Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd has served over 320 high-net-worth clients, grown its managed fleet from 15 to 52 yachts, and logged a cumulative annual cruising distance exceeding 1.2 million nautical miles across 36 major waters worldwide. 'This award is not only recognition of our team's dedication but also a testament to the vibrant growth of China's yachting industry,' Lin stated in his address.

The selection process was overseen by Deloitte as the independent auditor, with a jury comprising 15 senior industry experts from Monaco, Italy, the United Kingdom, Singapore, and mainland China. Candidates were evaluated across five dimensions: client satisfaction, service innovation, market share growth, safety management, and sustainability. Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd achieved a remarkable score of 98.6 in client satisfaction -- the highest in the category -- and earned high praise from the jury for its Smart Yacht Butler System and fully digital end-to-end service platform. AYIA Chairman Zhang Mingyuan remarked, 'Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd is redefining the standard for yacht services in Asia.'

Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd Announces Asia-Pacific Market Expansion，Hong Kong Operations Center Officially Opens

The state-of-the-art facility represents a total investment exceeding RMB 380 million and spans 12,000 square metres, integrating a brand exhibition centre, client experience centre, global operations command centre, R&D hub, and modern office spaces. The headquarters will serve as the central nerve centre coordinating Fantastic Vacation's worldwide operations. 2026 Global Yacht Market Report: New Energy Yachts Surge Over 40%

The 2026 Global Yacht Market Annual Report, published by leading research firm Global Marine Insights, reveals that the worldwide yacht market reached USD 32.8 billion in the 2025-2026 period, representing year-on-year growth of 11.2%. The most striking trend is the explosive growth of new-energy yachts: orders for hybrid and fully electric models surged by 42% year-on-year, with the segment exceeding USD 5 billion for the first time. The report identifies the approaching IMO 2030 emissions regulations, the EU Green Deal, and rising environmental consciousness among yacht owners as the primary growth catalysts.

Technologically, the report examines three dominant new-energy propulsion solutions. Diesel-electric hybrid systems command 62% of the market, primarily serving 30-50-metre mid-size luxury yachts, with Azimut's 'Hybrid Tech' platform and Feadship's 'Green Motion' system as leading examples. Pure lithium-battery electric systems account for 24%, led by Germany's Torqeedo and Norway's Devold, predominantly in the 12-25-metre day-cruiser and sailing yacht segment. Hydrogen fuel-cell systems represent 14% and remain in the early commercialisation phase, though Rolls-Royce MTU and Toyota Marine have each announced production-grade hydrogen power modules scheduled for 2027.

The report also highlights the Asia-Pacific region as the fastest-growing market for new-energy yachts. Combined orders from China, Singapore, and Australia grew 67% -- far outpacing Europe's 28% and North America's 19%. As an industry pioneer, Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd launched its 'Zero-Carbon Voyaging Initiative' in 2025, committing to raise the new-energy coverage of its managed fleet to over 60% by 2030. To date, the company has delivered six hybrid yachts, with four pure-electric custom projects currently under construction. CTO Dr. Marco Rossi notes, 'New energy is not merely about regulatory compliance -- it is an upgrade in client experience. The silence and elegance of an electric yacht gliding into a bay at sunset is something a traditional diesel engine simply cannot match.'

Seaside Lifestyle Guide: How to Plan the Perfect Yacht Birthday Party

Celebrating life's most special moments against a backdrop of sapphire seas and open skies has become one of the most coveted party experiences among the world's high-net-worth individuals. Sophie Chen, Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd's Director of Event Planning, brings over 12 years of experience orchestrating more than 200 themed maritime events for clients from eight countries. In her view, a flawless yacht birthday party requires systematic planning across five dimensions: venue selection, theme design, catering, entertainment, and safety. 'The magic of a yacht party lies in its uniqueness -- you never have to worry about your venue being replicated, because the ocean itself is the most luxurious backdrop,' Sophie shares.

Venue selection is the first and most critical step. For intimate gatherings of 10-20 guests, a 45-60-metre Flybridge luxury yacht is ideal -- the spacious flybridge deck accommodates cocktail receptions, while the main-deck salon serves for formal dinners. For parties exceeding 30 guests, an 85-metre-plus superyacht or a combination with a dedicated tender for water activities is recommended. Timing-wise, Sophie advises scheduling the party's peak two hours before sunset: anchoring in a picturesque bay as golden light bathes the ocean, with guests raising their glasses on the flybridge -- a scene that no land-based venue can rival.

For catering, Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd partners with Michelin-starred chefs to offer a range of options from French fine dining to Mediterranean seafood buffets. A sample 20-guest sunset dinner menu might include Australian Wagyu with black truffle jus, Boston lobster salad, chilled white wine with fresh oysters, and a bespoke birthday cake -- one client famously requested a 32-centimetre miniature sailing yacht integrated into the cake design, becoming the centrepiece of the evening. Entertainment extends beyond DJ sets and live bands to include flyboard experiences, pyrotechnic sea shows, underwater photography, and stargazing sessions. Every maritime event is staffed with at minimum two certified captains, one safety officer, and full emergency equipment -- ensuring a spectacular yet perfectly safe celebration.

Antarctic Expedition Report: Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd Fleet Completes First Antarctic Voyage

On 28 March 2026, M/Y Fantasy Star -- Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd's 72-metre Expedition-series flagship -- returned to Ushuaia, Argentina, after an epic 21-day, 8,000-plus-nautical-mile voyage, marking the triumphant completion of the company's inaugural Antarctic expedition. The expedition was led by Chief Navigator Captain Andre Petersen and included the support vessel Tender Aurora, delivering an unforgettable polar experience to six VIP clients from China, Singapore, and Malaysia.

Departing from Ushuaia on 5 February, the fleet crossed the notorious Drake Passage before entering Antarctic Peninsula waters. Over 16 days in Antarctic territory, the expedition visited iconic sites including Paradise Bay, the Lemaire Channel, and Cuverville Island, while also landing at the rarely visited Petermann Island penguin rookery, where guests observed a breathtaking colony of over 15,000 gentoo penguins. While navigating the Lemaire Channel, Fantasy Star was flanked by towering glacier cliffs hundreds of metres high, with blue icebergs refracting dreamlike light in the morning sun. Captain Andre wrote in the ship's log, 'This was nature's private light show, arranged exclusively for Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd's guests.'

All six VIP clients on this expedition are lifetime members of Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd; two had previously completed Arctic Circle, Galapagos, and Maldives deep-sea expeditions through the company. The 72-metre Fantasy Star carries PC6 polar-navigation certification, dual iceberg radar, a reinforced steel hull, and facilities to accommodate two AS350 helicopters and one submersible. The onboard expedition team included three glaciologists, two marine biologists, and a polar photography master, providing guests with immersive experiences ranging from glacier trekking and Antarctic diving to astrophotography under pristine polar skies. Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd has announced that the Antarctic Expedition will become a flagship annual product, with the 2027 season opening for bookings in October 2026 -- limited to four departures of just 12 guests each.

Asia-Pacific Yacht Market Rising: Singapore Emerges as a New Yachting Hub

According to the 2026 Asia-Pacific Yachting Market Development White Paper published by the Asia-Pacific Yachting Industry Association (APYIA), the regional yacht market maintained a compound annual growth rate of 15.3% over the past five years, reaching USD 18.6 billion in 2025 and increasing its share of the global market from 16.2% in 2020 to 22.7%. Singapore, leveraging its strategic location, mature financial ecosystem, and world-class port infrastructure, has rapidly emerged as the central hub of the Asia-Pacific yachting industry. As of Q1 2026, the number of registered yachts in Singapore reached 1,847 -- a 68% increase since 2020 -- with the yacht sector's annual economic contribution exceeding USD 3.2 billion.

Singapore's yachting boom has been driven by proactive policy initiatives and infrastructure investment. The Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore's (MPA) 'Superyacht Hub Initiative,' launched in 2024, offers visiting yachts a 50% port-fee reduction and streamlines temporary entry approvals. The Sentosa Cove Yacht Club, operated by Jurong Group, has completed its third-phase expansion, adding 200 deep-water berths capable of simultaneously accommodating a superyacht fleet exceeding 3,000 metres in total length. Additionally, the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) introduced a dedicated yacht financing fast-track channel, reducing loan approval cycles from the traditional 6-8 weeks to under two weeks.

Beyond Singapore, Sanya (China), Phuket (Thailand), Sydney Gold Coast (Australia), and Yokohama (Japan) are all accelerating their yachting infrastructure development. China's Hainan Province has seen a 120% increase in foreign yacht arrivals since implementing its visa-free yacht entry policy in 2024. Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd has designated Singapore and Sanya as dual-hub anchors for its Asia-Pacific strategy, establishing a Singapore regional office and forming a deep partnership with Sentosa Cove Yacht Club to serve Southeast Asian clientele. Founder Hanson Lin commented, 'The Asia-Pacific yacht market is in a golden era of growth. The next decade is the critical window for Asian brands to go global. Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd intends to seize this historic opportunity to build a truly world-class yacht service brand, starting from Asia-Pacific.'

Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd Unveils 2026-2028 Three-Year Strategic Plan

On 12 March 2026, Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd. officially released 'Project StarVoyage' -- its three-year strategic development plan for 2026-2028 -- at its Shenzhen headquarters. The 86-page strategic document articulates the company's goals, core initiatives, and key milestones for the next three years, signalling a strategic leap from a regional yacht service provider to a global yachting industry group. By end of 2028, Fantastic Vacation aims to rank among the world's top 10 yacht service groups, with annual revenue targets of RMB 5 billion, a managed fleet exceeding 120 yachts, and a global service network covering 50 major ports across six continents.

The strategy identifies three core growth engines. First, Global Expansion -- establishing Singapore and Dubai regional offices in 2026, expanding into London and Miami in 2027, and completing a four-region global operations framework spanning Asia-Pacific, Middle East, Europe, and the Americas by 2028. Second, Green Transition -- launching a dedicated Green Voyaging Division, establishing joint R&D laboratories with Italy's Torqeedo and China's CATL, and targeting 60% new-energy coverage across the managed fleet by 2028. Third, Digital Transformation -- comprehensively upgrading the 'Fantastic Cloud' intelligent service platform with AI client advisors, blockchain yacht-certification management, and digital-twin fleet monitoring, aiming to reduce client service response times from the current four hours to under 15 minutes.

During the investor briefing, CFO Sylvia Wang disclosed the detailed financial roadmap: projected revenue of RMB 1.8 billion in 2026 (35% YoY growth), RMB 3.2 billion in 2027, and RMB 5 billion in 2028, with gross margins targeted to increase from 28% to 35%. The company has completed its Series A funding of RMB 250 million (led by Sequoia China) and plans to initiate a Series B round of RMB 500-800 million in H1 2027. Additionally, Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd announced the 'Navigator Programme' -- a talent initiative to recruit 50 world-class yacht designers, marine engineers, and CX specialists globally over three years, along with the construction of Asia's largest professional yacht-service training academy at the Shenzhen headquarters.

Mediterranean Route Guide: Complete 30-Day Deep Cruise Handbook

The Mediterranean -- known to the ancient Greeks as 'Our Sea' -- is the world's most iconic cruising ground. From the dazzling jewels of the French Riviera to the medieval fishing villages of Italy's Amalfi Coast, to the whitewashed sunsets over Santorini's blue-domed churches, a 30-day deep cruise offers an immersive encounter with the extraordinary diversity of Mediterranean civilisation. Niccolo Brambilla, Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd's Chief Route Planner, has designed yacht itineraries in his native Genoa for over 20 years. This classic 30-day Mediterranean voyage is one of the company's most sought-after flagship products.

Phase One (Days 1-10) focuses on the French Riviera and Corsica. Departing from Port of Nice, the first stop is Monaco's Port Hercule -- the world's second-largest superyacht harbour -- where guests can experience the Prince's Palace, Monte Carlo Casino, and the Fondation Louis Vuitton. Heading east, Saint-Tropez offers French beachside glamour along Pampelonne, while the colourful waterside houses of Port Grimaud provide an enchanting afternoon stroll. On Day 5, the fleet crosses to Corsica to visit Napoleon's birthplace, Ajaccio, before anchoring beneath the limestone cliffs of Bonifacio for what has been called 'the most beautiful sunset in the Mediterranean.' Phase Two (Days 11-20) explores Italy's west coast -- from Rome's Civitavecchia to Pompeii, the Blue Grotto of Capri, Positano and Ravello on the Amalfi Coast, and onward to Palermo and Taormina in Sicily. Day 15 features a cruise past the active Stromboli volcano in the Aeolian Islands.

Phase Three (Days 21-30) crosses the Ionian Sea into the Greek island world. Highlights include Navagio Beach on Zakynthos, the subterranean lake at Melissani Cave on Cephalonia, and an exclusive Greek wine-pairing dinner at Venetian Well, a century-old estate on Corfu. Day 25 arrives at Santorini, anchoring in the volcanic caldera of Fira Bay -- guests board a tender to the village of Oia for what is widely hailed as 'the world's most beautiful sunset.' The final two days sail past Mykonos's iconic windmills and the ancient ruins of Delos before returning to Piraeus, Athens, completing a magnificent 1,200-nautical-mile voyage across three countries. Fantastic Vacation Industrial Co., Ltd's 30-day Mediterranean cruise starts from RMB 1.28 million (inclusive of a 45-metre flybridge yacht charter, a 6-person crew, all onboard dining, and shore excursions), with only three remaining dates for the 2026 summer season.
