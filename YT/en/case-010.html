core service highlights

🏝️ indigenous tribe visits

Visiting Vanuatu's indigenous tribes, where guests experience native culture, traditional ceremonies, and craftsmanship — accompanied throughout by local guides.

🤿 world-class diving

Fiji and Vanuatu boast world-class dive sites, supported by PADI instructors and professional diving equipment for exploring coral reefs and WWII shipwrecks.

🚁 helicopter expedition

The yacht carries a helicopter enabling guests to take in volcanic landscapes, rainforests, and islands from above — a true land-sea-air three-dimensional expedition.

🛶 primitive kayaking

A professional kayaking team guides guests through mangrove forests and sea caves — exploring hidden waterways inaccessible to the yacht.

🍳 Michelin at sea

A Michelin-starred chef fuses South Pacific ingredients with Asian culinary techniques for a truly unique maritime gastronomic experience.

📸 professional photography service

Underwater and aerial photographers document the entire expedition — delivering a beautifully produced expedition documentary and photo album.
